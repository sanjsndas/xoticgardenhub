    🌿 Soil Science: The Living Foundation

    Healthy soil is 45% minerals, 25% water, 25% air, and 5% organic matter – but the magic lies in the microorganisms. In this 3,500-word chapter, we break down NPK ratios, biochar application, composting methods (hot vs cold), vermiculture, and mycorrhizal networks. A single teaspoon of quality garden soil contains over 1 billion bacteria. Understanding this allows you to feed the soil, not just the plant.

    We also cover pH balancing for acid-loving plants like azaleas and blueberries, versus alkaline-tolerant lavender and clematis. With 14 DIY soil tests and remediation strategies, you'll never face nutrient lockout again.
