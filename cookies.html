🌿 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. They help websites remember your actions and preferences (such as login, language, font size, and other display preferences) over a period of time, so you don’t have to keep re-entering them whenever you come back or browse from one page to another. Cookies can be “persistent” (remain after you close your browser) or “session” (deleted when you close your browser).

🌱 Gardening analogy: Think of cookies like plant markers in a botanical garden — they help us recognize your favorite section, remember your preferred soil tips, and give you a seamless journey through our lush content.

🌱 Frequently Asked Questions About Cookies

❓ Do cookies collect my personal information like name or email?

No. Standard cookies cannot read your personal files or capture sensitive data such as your name, address, or payment details. However, if you are logged into your account (future member area), cookies help maintain your session but they do not expose your credentials. For more details, refer to our Privacy Policy.

❓ Can I browse Xotic Garden Hub without cookies?

Absolutely! You can set your browser to block all cookies, but some features may not work as seamlessly — for example, the plant identifier tool might not remember your recent searches. The core reading experience, however, remains fully accessible.

❓ Does Xotic Garden Hub use cookies for advertising retargeting?

We may use limited, privacy-friendly retargeting cookies only if you have explicitly consented. For instance, if you looked at a page about rare orchids, you might see a subtle non-intrusive ad about our orchid webinar. You can opt out at any time via our preference center.

❓ How often do you audit your cookies?

We perform a quarterly cookie scan to ensure only necessary and declared cookies are active. Our commitment to transparency includes publishing the list of cookies in our developer portal upon request.
