🍪 3. Cookies Policy

Xotic Garden Hub uses cookies sparingly — because we respect your digital footprint just as we respect earthworms in compost. This policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can manage them.

🔍 3.1 What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your actions and preferences (like language or region) over a period of time.

🌿 3.2 Our Cookie Categories

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for navigation and core features (e.g., remembering your cookie consent choice, session security). These cannot be disabled.
  • Functional/Preference Cookies: We set a cookie to remember if you've dismissed a notification, or your preferred layout (grid/list view for plant guides). They expire after 30 days.
  • Analytics Cookies (First-Party Only): We use a privacy-forward analytics tool (no Google Analytics; we self-host Plausible or similar) that does not use persistent cookies to track individuals. It counts page views and referrers anonymously. No cross-site tracking or fingerprinting.
  • No Advertising/Tracking Cookies: We run zero third-party ads and no retargeting pixels. Your browsing is not sold to marketers.

⚙️ 3.3 Managing Cookie Preferences

Most browsers allow you to control cookies via settings. You can block or delete cookies, but disabling necessary cookies may affect site functionality (e.g., login for seed exchange). To adjust, go to your browser's "Privacy & Security" section. For more info, visit aboutcookies.org.

By continuing to use Xotic Garden Hub without changing your cookie settings, you consent to our use of functional and analytics cookies as described. However, we provide a simple "Cookie Preferences" pop-up on first visit.

🌾 3.4 Updates to this Cookies Policy
