🔒 Privacy Policy

Your personal data is as precious as a rare orchid. At Xotic Garden Hub, we are committed to transparency, security, and respect for your privacy. Read how we cultivate trust.

📅 Last updated: April 15, 2025

Welcome to Xotic Garden Hub (“we”, “our”, “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website xoticgardenhub.com (the “Site”), use our services, or interact with us through our gardening community. We believe in nurturing both plants and privacy — your data will never be sold or exploited. Please read this policy carefully. By accessing or using our Site, you agree to the terms described below.

🌱 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better gardening guidance, improve user experience, and respond to your inquiries. The types of data include:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address (when you fill out our contact form, request a consultation, or subscribe to newsletters).
  • Gardening Preferences: Information about your garden type, plant interests, growing zones, and feedback you share with our botanists.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and browsing patterns via cookies (see our Cookies Policy).
  • Communication Data: Any messages, photos, or documents you send via email, WhatsApp, or our contact forms for plant diagnosis or support.

🌸 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information only for legitimate purposes rooted in our gardening mission:

  • ✔ Respond to your inquiries, provide personalized gardening advice, and schedule consultations.
  • ✔ Process workshop registrations, event bookings, and plant orders (where applicable).
  • ✔ Improve our website content, recommend relevant articles, and send occasional newsletters (you can opt out anytime).
  • ✔ Enhance security, prevent fraud, and ensure the technical functionality of our platform.
  • ✔ Analyze aggregate trends to understand how gardeners interact with our resources.
🌻 Our promise: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. Any sharing is strictly limited to essential service providers (e.g., email delivery, payment processors) bound by confidentiality.

🍃 3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR & Indian IT Act compliance)

For visitors from the European Economic Area or under Indian data protection laws, we rely on the following legal bases: your consent (when you submit a form), performance of a contract (e.g., when you register for a paid workshop), legitimate interests (improving our services and responding to gardening queries), and compliance with legal obligations.

🌿 4. Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your information only in limited scenarios:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who assist in operating our website (hosting, analytics) or delivering services (courier partners for plant delivery). They are contractually obligated to protect your data.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental regulation to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Business Transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your data may be transferred with notice.

We do not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

📧 5. Your Rights & Choices

You have control over your personal data. Depending on your location, you may request:

  • 🔹 Access: Receive a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • 🔹 Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • 🔹 Deletion: Request erasure of your personal data (subject to exceptions like ongoing customer service).
  • 🔹 Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails via the “unsubscribe” link or by contacting us.

To exercise your rights, please email us at privacy@xoticgardenhub.com or use the contact form on our Contact page. We will respond within 30 days.

🛡️ 6. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard measures including SSL encryption, restricted access, and regular security audits. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy — typically 2 years after your last interaction, or longer if required by law (e.g., transaction records). Anonymous, aggregated data may be kept indefinitely for analytical insights.

🍪 7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience, remember preferences, and analyze site traffic. For detailed information about the types of cookies we use and how to manage them, please refer to our separate Cookies Policy. By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described.

👧 8. Children’s Privacy

Xotic Garden Hub is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we learn that we have inadvertently gathered such data, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians may contact us to request removal.

🌍 9. International Data Transfers

Our operations are based in India, but we may use servers or service providers located in other countries. By using our Site, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to jurisdictions with different data protection laws. We ensure appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place to protect your information.

📝 10. Third-Party Links

Our content may include links to external gardening resources, social media platforms, or partner nurseries. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third-party sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing any personal data.

🔔 11. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the latest revision. Material changes will be notified via a banner on our website or email (if you have subscribed). Your continued use of the Site after modifications constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

📖 Summary of Your Key Privacy Rights

Right to be informed: You have the right to know what data we collect and why — this policy gives you that clarity.
Right to access & portability: Request a machine-readable copy of your data.
Right to rectification: Fix any inaccurate details (e.g., misspelled name or outdated address).
Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”): Ask us to delete your data when it’s no longer necessary.
Right to object/restrict processing: You can limit how we use your data, especially for direct marketing.
Right to lodge a complaint: If you feel your privacy rights are violated, you may file a complaint with local supervisory authority (e.g., Indian Data Protection Board).
